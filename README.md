# DnD GM Companion App

A companion app for Dungeon Masters / Game Masters running Dungeons & Dragons (5e) sessions. The goal is to take the bookkeeping off the GM's plate so they can focus on the story and the players at the table.

> **Status:** Early planning. Nothing to install yet.

## Vision

One place to prep a session, run it live, and record what happened afterwards, without juggling a dozen browser tabs, notebooks and spreadsheets.

## Planned Features (draft)

- **Campaign & session notes**: sessions, NPCs, locations, factions and plot threads, all linked to each other
- **Initiative & combat tracker**: turn order, HP, conditions, concentration and round counters
- **Party overview**: AC, passive Perception/Insight, HP and key stats for every player at a glance
- **Quick reference**: rules, conditions and spell/monster lookups (SRD content)
- **Generators**: random names, loot, encounters and tavern patrons
- **Session log**: timestamped notes during play that turn into a recap afterwards
